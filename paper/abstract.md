# KDS-EL 论文摘要（双语文稿）

> 目标期刊 Applied Soft Computing（Elsevier，纯英文期刊）。主稿使用下方英文摘要（约 230 词，符合 ASOC 摘要字数要求）。中文版仅作作者参考/中文论文复用，不进入英文主稿。

## 英文摘要（用于论文 main.tex）

Feature selection in high-dimensional data, where the number of features vastly exceeds the number of samples, remains a central challenge for evolutionary computation. Generic particle swarm optimization (PSO) suffers from three structural deficiencies in this setting: random initialization wastes search budget in sparse solution spaces, uniform mutation discards cross-iteration feature-importance signals, and fixed objective weights are misaligned with the distinct phases of the search. This paper proposes KDS-EL, a knowledge-guided evolutionary learning method that builds upon a multi-task PSO backbone and integrates three complementary mechanisms. Mutual-information-guided initialization with adaptive re-initialization supplies informed starting points and escapes local optima on stagnation. A Knowledge-guided Elite Refinement (KER) strategy accumulates a cross-iteration feature-weight matrix governed by exponential decay and flips elite solutions toward historically effective features at zero additional classifier evaluations. A Two-Phase Weight Scheduling strategy partitions the search into an aggressive compression phase followed by an accuracy-refinement phase. On 20 high-dimensional benchmark datasets spanning gene expression, text, image, and speech domains, KDS-EL is compared against 13 competitors, including five single-objective PSO variants and five multi-objective feature selection methods. KDS-EL ranks first with a mean accuracy of 0.9574 and only 87.8 selected features on average, outperforming the strongest single-objective and multi-objective competitors by +3.97 and +4.42 percentage points, respectively, while delivering a 3.4$\times$ speedup over the MEL backbone. Wilcoxon signed-rank tests confirm superiority over MEL on 20/20 datasets, and Holm--Bonferroni correction establishes statistical significance against all 13 competitors. The component contribution analysis shows that the three mechanisms yield cumulative gains of +7.06 percentage points in accuracy and compress the feature count from 2{,}722 to 88.

## 中文摘要（仅供作者参考）

高维特征选择中，特征数量远超样本数量，这一问题对进化计算构成持续挑战。通用粒子群优化（PSO）在此场景下存在三个结构性缺陷：随机初始化在稀疏解空间中浪费搜索预算、均匀变异丢弃跨代际的特征重要性信号、固定目标权重与搜索的不同阶段相错位。本文提出 KDS-EL，一种以多任务 PSO 为主干、集成三种互补机制的知识引导进化学习方法。互信息引导初始化与自适应再初始化提供信息化的起点，并在停滞时逃离局部最优；知识引导精英精化（KER）策略通过指数衰减的跨代际特征权重矩阵，以零额外分类器评估代价将精英解向历史有效特征翻转；两阶段权重调度策略将搜索划分为激进的压缩阶段与精度精化阶段。在跨越基因表达、文本、图像与语音四个领域的 20 个高维基准数据集上，KDS-EL 与 13 个对比方法（含 5 个单目标 PSO 变体与 5 个多目标特征选择方法）进行比较。KDS-EL 以 0.9574 的平均精度与仅 87.8 个平均选中特征排名第一，分别领先最强的单目标与多目标竞争对手 3.97 与 4.42 个百分点，同时相对 MEL 主干实现 3.4 倍加速。Wilcoxon 符号秩检验确认其在 20/20 数据集上优于 MEL，Holm-Bonferroni 校正证明其对全部 13 个对比方法具有统计显著性。组件贡献分析表明，三种机制累计带来 +7.06 个百分点精度提升，并将特征数从 2,722 压缩至 88。
