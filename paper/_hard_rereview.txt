Highlights
Multi-Task Particle Swarm Optimization with Knowledge-Guided Elite Refinement and Two-
Phase Weight Scheduling for High-Dimensional Feature Selection

  • Knowledge-guided dual-swarm framework for high-dimensional feature selection.

  • MI soft prior seeding and adaptive recovery mitigate search stagnation.

  • Differential importance memory with exponential decay steers elite refinement.

  • Dynamic two-phase scheduling decouples coarse pruning from fine-grained tuning.

  • Attains 95.45% internal benchmark CV accuracy with 87.8 features and 3.4x speedup.

5. Conclusion

     In this paper, KDS-EL is proposed to address search blindness,
evolutionary amnesia, destructive perturbation, and static weighting in
ultra-high-dimensional wrapper feature selection. By integrating mutual
information prior initialization with adaptive recovery, KDS-EL establishes
informative search basins while safeguarding diversity. To mitigate destructive
perturbation, the KER strategy harvests generational fitness differentials under
exponential memory decay, steering targeted refinement within bounded
computation. Furthermore, dynamic Two-Phase Weight Scheduling temporally
decouples coarse pruning from fine accuracy refinement. Comprehensive
evaluations confirm that KDS-EL achieves competitive multi-criteria perfor-
mance, attaining 0.9545 mean internal benchmark cross-validation accuracy,
contracting feature subsets to an average of 87.8 dimensions, and achieving
a 3.4-fold acceleration over canonical dual-swarm search under the reported
implementation environment. The discovered compact subsets demonstrate
exploratory cross-model transferability across Support Vector Machines
and Random Forest models on representative benchmarks, while isolating
compact candidate gene subsets of 39 to 83 dimensions showing exploratory
retrospective concordance with characterized oncological literature.
     Several promising trajectories emerge for future exploration. First, inte-
grating self-supervised embeddings could uncover higher-order epistatic inter-
actions. Second, cross-cohort transfer mechanisms could enable reusing ac-
cumulated importance memories. Third, extending the proposed knowledge-
guided components, including mutual information initialization and differential
elite refinement, into an explicit multi-objective Pareto framework represents
